Raag Smriti

Real time Classical Music radio obeying the Hindustani Time-theory, A Raag is the melodic framework in Hindustani classical music, structured using specific notes (swaras) and rules to create a distinct emotional mood (rasa) that colors the mind. Samay ka Siddhant (Time Theory) dictates that certain ragas are performed at specific times of the day or seasons, aligning musical, emotional, and atmospheric energy
